Honoring the Legacy of Harold & Kay Andresen

About Harold & Kay Andresen

The Andresen Memorial Scholarships were established to honor the memory and legacy of Harold and Kay Andresen. Their dedication to education and community has inspired the creation of these scholarships to help future generations pursue their dreams.

Two scholarships are offered: the Andresen Memorial Ag Scholarship for students pursuing agriculture-related fields, and the Andresen Memorial Scholarship for students in other areas of study.

Available Scholarships

  • Andresen Memorial Ag Scholarship

    For students pursuing studies in agriculture, farming, agricultural business, animal science, agronomy, or related fields. This scholarship honors Harold's lifelong commitment to agriculture and farming.

    Learn More & Apply

  • Andresen Memorial Scholarship

    For students pursuing studies in any non-agriculture field. This scholarship supports students in their educational journey across various disciplines.

    Learn More & Apply
