Andresen Memorial Ag Scholarship

The Andresen Memorial Ag Scholarship was established to support students who are passionate about agriculture and wish to pursue a career in farming, agricultural business, or related fields. This scholarship honors Harold Andresen's lifelong dedication to agriculture and his belief in the importance of supporting the next generation of agricultural leaders.

Eligibility Requirements

  • Must be a graduating high school senior or current college student
  • Must be pursuing or planning to pursue a degree in an agriculture-related field such as:
    • Agriculture
    • Animal Science
    • Agronomy
    • Agricultural Business
    • Farm Management
    • Agricultural Education
    • Other related agricultural fields
  • Must demonstrate academic achievement and commitment to their field of study
  • Must demonstrate financial need or merit

Application Requirements

  • Completed application form
  • Personal essay describing your interest in agriculture and career goals
  • Official transcript
  • One letter of recommendation

Award Amount

The scholarship amount varies based on available funds and the number of qualified applicants.
