Andresen Ag Scholarship

Encouraging the next generation of Carroll County farmers and agriculturalists.

About the Scholarship

Agriculture is a vital part of our global economy, and right now as the world faces more and more complex challenges, the demand for motivated, innovative individuals is higher than ever. Working in agriculture offers many diverse career opportunities that are exciting, challenging and incredibly rewarding.

Harold and Kay Andresen of rural Chadwick, Illinois farmed for over 50 years. In appreciation of their success in farming and in their memory, the Andresen family awards Ag Scholarships to encourage young people to join the field of agriculture. Kay passed away in 2017 and Harold in 2025, but their legacy of supporting the next generation continues.

Award Amount

$1,500 scholarships are being awarded to graduating seniors who have been accepted at an accredited community college, university or technical school.

Selection Criteria

The scholarships are based on:

  • Financial need of the student
  • Scholastic ability
  • Moral character
  • Leadership qualities
  • Community involvement

There will be an unlimited number of Ag scholarships given out to deserving young people in Carroll County.

Application Requirements

Please include with the application:

  • General & Academic Information
  • High School Transcript
  • Family Financial Information
  • An Essay: In 300 words or less, please describe your plans for your education, long-term and short-term goals, and what difference your education would make to you, your family and your community.
  • One Teacher Recommendation
