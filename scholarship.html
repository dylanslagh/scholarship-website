Andresen Memorial Scholarship

Honoring Randy and Rodney Andresen, and the legacy of a family that gives back.

About the Scholarship

Every year since 1985, the Andresen Family of rural Chadwick has awarded the Randy and Rodney Andresen Memorial scholarships to graduating high school seniors. Even though they were afflicted with muscular dystrophy, they still attended school and graduated with honors in 1979 and 1980. They worked hard, rarely complained and were truly an inspiration to their family, friends, and classmates.

In 2012, the family decided to rename the scholarships the Andresen Memorial Scholarship, still in honor of Randy and Rodney. With the passing of Kay in 2017 and Harold in 2025, the scholarships now also honor their memory and the legacy of generosity they built together.

Award Amount

$1,500 scholarships are awarded to selected applicants — graduating high school seniors from the three Carroll County high schools who have been accepted at an accredited community college, university or technical school.

Selection Criteria

The scholarships are based on:

  • Scholastic ability
  • Moral character
  • Leadership qualities
  • Community involvement

There will be an unlimited number of scholarships given out to deserving young people in Carroll County.

Application Requirements

Please include with the application:

  • General & Academic Information
  • High School Transcript
  • An Essay: In 300 words or less, please describe your plans for your education, long-term and short-term goals, and what difference your education would make to you, your family and your community.
  • One Teacher Recommendation
