Andresen Memorial Scholarship

The Andresen Memorial Scholarship was established to support students pursuing higher education in any field outside of agriculture. This scholarship honors Harold and Kay Andresen's belief in the transformative power of education and their commitment to helping young people achieve their dreams.

Eligibility Requirements

  • Must be a graduating high school senior or current college student
  • Must be pursuing or planning to pursue a degree in a non-agriculture field such as:
    • Business
    • Education
    • Healthcare & Nursing
    • Engineering
    • Arts & Humanities
    • Sciences
    • Technology
    • Any other non-agriculture field
  • Must demonstrate academic achievement and commitment to their field of study
  • Must demonstrate financial need or merit

Application Requirements

  • Completed application form
  • Personal essay describing your educational goals and career aspirations
  • Official transcript
  • One letter of recommendation

Award Amount

The scholarship amount varies based on available funds and the number of qualified applicants.
